Know your ballot like you know your fantasy roster

Who they are. What they did. What they plan.

Stump League turns candidates' public records into stat lines you can read in five minutes: whether they face voters, who funds them, whether they show up, and whether they get things done. Every number links to its source. There's no overall grade, and nothing here tells you who to vote for.

    About this prototype

    An open proof of concept

    Stump League is an AI-assisted prototype of a nonpartisan way to present candidates' public records the way sports media presents players. It's open source so people with more technical and political expertise, including political science students, can adopt it, check it and build on it.

    What's real today
      What isn't
      • The Fictional State 9th District race, and the whole fantasy league. Both are invented and labeled.
      • No human has reviewed anything yet. Official records are imported automatically; everything that took judgment is marked Draft.
      • Questionnaires, town halls, disclosures, state campaign finance and Arkansas legislature votes aren't collected yet.

      For builders

      The code is MIT-licensed: anyone can copy, change and build on it. The data pipeline runs nightly on GitHub Actions for free, and the methodology is published in full. Best-fit homes are nonpartisan: university research centers, civic-tech groups, nonprofit newsrooms and voter-information organizations.

      View the code and contributor guide

      Commitments
      • No overall grades for real candidates, no endorsements, no "vote for" language
      • No scoring of policy positions or how anyone voted on an issue
      • No party colors; party appears as text only
      • Corrections in public, through GitHub issues
      • Free to play; no money, ever
