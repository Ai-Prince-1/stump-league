About this prototype

An open proof of concept

Stump League is a working prototype of a nonpartisan platform that presents candidates' records the way sports media presents players. It is built to be adopted and extended by an established civic, academic or newsroom partner.

What's real today
  • Ohio's governor race as a second pilot, showing how three first-time candidates look as scouting reports.
  • Every contested race on Arkansas's Nov 3 ballot (50 races, 102 candidates), the 80 unopposed seats, and plain-language explainers for the 4 statewide ballot issues.
  • Official data refreshed nightly: congressional and state legislative effectiveness scores, House and Senate roll calls, Congress.gov bills, FEC campaign money for every Arkansas federal candidate, fact-checks, and the Census ZIP-to-district map.
  • Every real-world fact links to a public source.
  • Promise ledgers and specificity checks are draft ratings, clearly labeled, awaiting review.
  • The scoring engine, tiers, sliders, head-to-head and lessons all work.
What's sample
  • The Fictional State 9th District race, built to show a fully populated stat sheet.
  • The entire fantasy league: players, teams and points.

From prototype to product

The code is the small part. A full product needs four things running every week: official data feeds, a roster of every candidate, reviewed promise ledgers, and a trust process people can check.

For partners

The methodology, data schema and code are designed to be open. A partner gets a working product, a documented method and a launch moment: every official elected on Nov 3 starts a fresh promise ledger in January.

Best-fit homes are nonpartisan: university research centers, civic-tech foundations, nonprofit newsrooms and voter-information organizations.

Commitments
  • No endorsements and no "vote for" language
  • No scoring of policy positions or how anyone voted on an issue
  • No party colors; party appears as text only
  • Public corrections log and candidate right of reply
  • Free to play; no money, ever
